These 5 different lamps are inspired by the Mid-Century Modern furniture style, and can be easily adapted to different projects since they are color and light customizable.

Approach

I modeled the lamps in Blender and created PBR material textures for each one so they could be color customizable. I proceeded to set up the lights and parent them with the respective lamps, so they could be adjustable depending on the desired effect.
